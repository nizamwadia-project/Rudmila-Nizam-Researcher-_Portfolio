Who I Am

Where Molecules
Meet Machines

I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering student at BUET — Bangladesh's most prestigious technical university — with a research profile that spans both the computational and the physical dimensions of medicine.

My work sits at an unusual intersection: I simulate drug–protein interactions at the molecular level using physics-based computational tools, while simultaneously designing and fabricating low-cost prosthetic hands for real patients in under-resourced settings. The thread connecting both is a belief that engineering can be precise, accessible, and deeply human.

My research has been recognized across three continents — from a poster at Carnegie Mellon to an Open Source Award at Rice University's global design competition. I am now seeking to deepen this work through graduate research in computational biomedical engineering or biomedical AI.

I believe the most transformative healthcare technologies are those that are both scientifically rigorous and economically equitable. That is the standard I hold my own work to.

⭐ Open Source Award Rice 360 · 2025
Sep 2024 – Feb 2025
Nitinol-Actuated Biomimetic Prosthetic Hand
A paradigm shift in low-cost prosthetics: instead of servo motors, this hand uses shape-memory alloy (Nitinol) wires that contract like muscle fibers when heated — enabling smooth, biomimetic finger motion with simpler mechanics and lower cost. Integrated flex sensor feedback enables adaptive grip modulation, yielding measurably better actuation efficiency than conventional servo-driven systems.
Nitinol SMA Arduino Flex Sensors 3D Printing Control Systems

Thesis Research Ongoing · 2025
Undergraduate Thesis
Hybrid Physics-ML Models for Drug Discovery
Investigating SARS-CoV-2 main protease inhibition through a combination of coarse-grained molecular dynamics and machine learning — building predictive models for drug–protein binding affinity that go beyond pure simulation or pure data-driven approaches. The goal: faster, more interpretable hit identification for antiviral drug pipelines.
LAMMPS CHARMM-GUI VMD PyRx Python Scikit-learn
🧬
2024 🫁
MFCC-Based Lung Disease Detection
Built an end-to-end respiratory sound analysis pipeline — extracting MFCC acoustic biomarkers from lung auscultation recordings and classifying disease states with KNN and CNN models. Targets early-stage detection in low-resource clinical settings where spirometry is unavailable.
MFCC KNN CNN Signal Processing
2024 🧪
ML-Based Colorimetric pH Recognition
Trained SVM, KNN, and neural network models to estimate pH levels from color images of indicator strips — enabling non-invasive, low-cost biochemical monitoring. Application in renal diagnostics where lab infrastructure is limited.
SVM Neural Networks Computer Vision Point-of-Care

Nitinol-Based Prosthetic Hand System
Designed, fabricated, and validated a shape-memory alloy actuated prosthetic system with Arduino-based control. Demonstrated superior actuation smoothness over servo-motor equivalents. Presented at Rice 360 and won the Open Source Award.
Jan 2024 – Jul 2024
3D-Printed Body-Powered Prosthetic Hand
Designed and optimized a low-cost body-powered prosthetic with elbow-cable actuation. Structural integrity improvements enabled practical use in rural clinical settings. Presented at IEEE WIE Conference, Chennai 2024.
